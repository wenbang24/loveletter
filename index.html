      How to play

      On your turn, play one of your two cards and keep the other. Resolve its effect, then the next player draws. Survive with the highest card when the deck runs out, or be the last player standing. Tied players each win a favor.

      Targets must be active and unprotected, except Prince can always target yourself. If no opponent is eligible, Guard, Priest, Baron, and King have no effect. Prince must still target yourself.

      Winners earn one favor. The only surviving player who played or discarded a Spy earns one extra favor. Scores carry into the next round while you stay in the room; there is no match-winning threshold.

      This version uses all 21 cards, with no reserved card. If a Prince needs a replacement after the deck is empty, that player keeps no card and the round ends. New arrivals watch until the next round.
